# Employee Onboarding Standard Operating Procedure

**Document ID:** SOP-EMP-001  
**Version:** 2.3  
**Last Updated:** 2024-01-20  
**Owner:** Human Resources & IT Operations

## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard process for onboarding new employees into the organization, ensuring consistent access provisioning, training completion, and tool enablement within the first 30 days of employment.

## Scope

Applies to all full-time and contract employees joining the organization.

## Onboarding Timeline

| Phase | Duration | Owner | Key Activities |
|-------|----------|-------|-----------------|
| **Pre-Arrival** | Day -5 to 0 | HR | Background check, offer acceptance, equipment order |
| **Day 1** | Day 1 | IT Ops | Hardware setup, network access, email provisioning |
| **Week 1** | Days 2–5 | HR + Department Lead | Orientation, policy training, team introductions |
| **Week 2–3** | Days 8–21 | Department Lead | Role-specific training, tool access, project assignment |
| **Week 4** | Days 22–30 | HR | 30-day check-in, feedback collection |

## Detailed Steps

### Step 1: Pre-Arrival Setup (HR)

**Responsible:** Human Resources  
**Timeline:** 5 days before start date

- [ ] Verify background check completion
- [ ] Confirm offer acceptance and start date
- [ ] Order laptop, monitor, peripherals
- [ ] Create employee record in HRIS system
- [ ] Prepare onboarding materials (handbook, org chart, benefits guide)
- [ ] Notify IT Ops of new hire

**Deliverable:** Onboarding checklist sent to employee and department lead

---

### Step 2: IT Infrastructure Setup (IT Operations)

**Responsible:** IT Operations  
**Timeline:** Day 1

- [ ] Provision laptop and peripherals
- [ ] Create network account and email address
- [ ] Configure VPN access
- [ ] Set up phone extension
- [ ] Provision access to shared drives and collaboration tools (Slack, Teams, etc.)
- [ ] Create employee record in Active Directory

**Deliverable:** IT access credentials and setup guide

---

### Step 3: Company Orientation (HR)

**Responsible:** Human Resources  
**Timeline:** Days 1–2

- [ ] Welcome meeting with HR
- [ ] Review company mission, values, and organizational structure
- [ ] Explain benefits, payroll, and HR policies
- [ ] Conduct mandatory compliance training (anti-harassment, data security, etc.)
- [ ] Distribute employee handbook and sign acknowledgment

**Deliverable:** Signed policy acknowledgments, completed compliance training

---

### Step 4: Department Onboarding (Department Lead)

**Responsible:** Department Lead  
**Timeline:** Days 2–5

- [ ] Introduce team members and key stakeholders
- [ ] Provide department overview and current projects
- [ ] Assign workspace and equipment
- [ ] Schedule 1:1 meetings with team members
- [ ] Clarify role responsibilities and success metrics

**Deliverable:** Role clarity document, team contact list

---

### Step 5: Tools & Systems Access (IT Operations + Department Lead)

**Responsible:** IT Operations, Department Lead  
**Timeline:** Days 5–10

- [ ] Provision access to department-specific systems
- [ ] **Atlas API Access:** If role requires API integration, provision API credentials and OAuth token (see [Atlas API v2 Documentation](./atlas-api-v2.md))
- [ ] Provide tool training and documentation links
- [ ] Set up code repository access (GitHub, GitLab, etc.)
- [ ] Configure development environment (IDEs, SDKs, databases)
- [ ] Assign security training for sensitive systems

**Deliverable:** Access credentials, tool setup guide, security acknowledgment

---

### Step 6: Role-Specific Training (Department Lead)

**Responsible:** Department Lead  
**Timeline:** Days 8–21

- [ ] Conduct role-specific training sessions
- [ ] Assign onboarding buddy or mentor
- [ ] Review standard operating procedures relevant to role
- [ ] Provide access to internal documentation and knowledge base
- [ ] Schedule shadowing sessions with experienced team members

**Deliverable:** Training completion sign-off, assigned projects

---

### Step 7: 30-Day Check-In (HR + Department Lead)

**Responsible:** Human Resources, Department Lead  
**Timeline:** Day 30

- [ ] Conduct 30-day feedback survey with employee
- [ ] Review progress against onboarding checklist
- [ ] Gather feedback from department lead
- [ ] Address any outstanding access or training needs
- [ ] Confirm successful integration into team

**Deliverable:** 30-day review summary, feedback notes

---

## Escalation

- **Missing IT access:** Contact IT Help Desk (ext. 5555)
- **Policy questions:** Contact HR (hr@company.com)
- **Tool/system issues:** Contact department lead or IT support
- **Urgent issues:** Escalate to IT Director or VP of HR
