# Incident Response SOP v2

**Version:** 2.0
**Status:** Current
**Released:** 2024-07-01

## Reporting

Create an incident record in the incident portal, notify the on-call engineer, and assign an incident commander for Sev-1 and Sev-2 events.

## Severity

Use Sev-1 for complete outage, Sev-2 for major customer impact, and Sev-3 for limited degradation.

## Communication

Publish a customer-facing status update every thirty minutes for Sev-1 incidents.

## Review

Complete a blameless post-incident review and create tracked action items within five business days.
