# Remote Work Policy v2

**Version:** 2.0
**Status:** Current
**Released:** 2024-08-01

## Eligibility

Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days each week with manager approval and team coverage planning.

## Security

Company laptop, approved VPN, multi-factor authentication, and annual security training are mandatory.

## Reporting

Employees record remote days in the workforce portal before the start of each week.

## Manager Actions

Managers must confirm coverage plans and escalate exceptions to People Operations.
