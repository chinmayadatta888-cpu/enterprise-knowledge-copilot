# Remote Work Policy v1

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Deprecated
**Last Updated:** 2024-01-10

## Eligibility

Employees may work remotely up to two days each week with manager approval.

## Security

Employees must use a company laptop and approved VPN for company systems.

## Reporting

Employees submit a weekly remote-work plan through their manager.

## Sunset Notice

This policy is replaced by Remote Work Policy v2 on 2024-08-01.
